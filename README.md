Sky Courier — Valley Run: a chase-view flight game. Fly the courier plane through 10 golden rings over a procedurally generated valley — patchwork fields, a winding river, pine forests and snow-capped ridges — then bring her home on runway 27 before the fuel runs out. Throttle and turn discipline matter: fly too slow and the wing stalls, fly too low and the terrain wins. A live HUD shows altitude, speed, heading, fuel and score, with a compass tape, altitude/speed tapes and an attitude indicator.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests: the game is plain static files under `game/`.

Controls: ↑ ↓ pitch, ← → bank, W / S throttle (or Shift / Ctrl), drag with mouse or finger to steer, Enter to fly, R to restart, P to pause, M to mute.
